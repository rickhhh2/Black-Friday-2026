## Before You Buy from Overseas

1. **Duty-free limit.** Under simplified clearance, goods from the US are duty-free up to $200, and goods from other countries up to $150. Some categories, such as health supplements, have their own rules. Check with [Korea Customs Service](https://www.customs.go.kr).
2. **Combined taxation.** Separate orders that land on the same day can be taxed together. With Black Friday volume, arrival dates overlap more than you'd expect.
3. **Personal Customs Clearance Code.** Many checkouts won't go through without one. Get it ahead of time on [UNI-PASS](https://unipass.customs.go.kr).
4. **Time zones.** US Black Friday deals usually open at midnight US Eastern, which is 2 p.m. in Korea.
5. **Spot fake discounts.** Some sellers raise prices just before Black Friday and then slap on "50% off." Check the usual price on Danawa or Fallcent first.
6. **Pay yearly for tools you already use.** If a subscription you rely on goes on sale, that's the time to lock in a year. [Seedance.kr](https://seedance.kr/en/black-friday-2026), for example, has annual plans at half price until November 30.

## FAQ

**When is Black Friday 2026?**
Friday, November 27, 2026, the day after US Thanksgiving (November 26). Cyber Monday is November 30.

**What time do Black Friday deals start in Korea?**
Most US stores open their deals at midnight US Eastern, which is 2 p.m. KST on November 27. Many, including Amazon and AliExpress, have been discounting since November 19.

**Do Korean stores do Black Friday?**
Yes, but earlier than the US. 11st, Gmarket and Olive Young wrapped up in early November. Coupang (until Nov 29), Musinsa (Nov 22 – Dec 2) and Ably (until Dec 3) are running now or starting soon.

**Do AI tools have Black Friday deals?**
It depends on the tool. General AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude usually don't discount for Black Friday. Video and image generators tend to offer bigger deals, and the AI video generator [Seedance.kr](https://seedance.kr/en) has 50% off annual plans until November 30.

**Does Steam have a Black Friday sale?**
Not a store-wide one. Steam runs a Black Friday deals hub from Nov 26 to Nov 30 (Pacific time), and the store-wide discounts come with the Winter Sale starting December 17.
